All things about Red Panda

What is a red panda?

Red pandas, also known as the lesser panda, is a mammal with reddish orange brown fur with a black belly and legs and a fluffy tail. They are mostly found in the Eastern Himalayas. Their long bushy tail is used to maintain balance and help keep them warm during the winter. Unfortunately, they are endangered species

Fun facts

  • They have false thumbs so they can grip onto bamboo to eat
  • Very similar to cats, they also lick themselves clean
  • With their sharp claws, they can climb down a tree head first
  • The white markings on their faces glow faintly, helping mothers find their lost cubs in the dark. The dark ‘tear tracks’ beneath their eyes also reduce sun glare.
  • Red panda have a sweet tooth and love eating apples. Zoos would give them apples as enrichment or rewards

Why are they endangered?

  1. Livestock Herding
  2. Free-roaming Dogs and Disease
  3. Poaching & Illegal Trade
  4. World Climate Change
  5. Bamboo Lifecycle
